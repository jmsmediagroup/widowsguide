The money

The Social Security death benefit: the one-time $255 payment

Who can get it, the two years you have to apply, and the other money that may be paid when a husband dies.

The lump-sum death payment: what it is

After a death, Social Security can make a one-time payment of $255. Its own name for it is the lump-sum death payment.

It is paid once. It is separate from any monthly survivor benefit on his record, which has to be asked for in its own right.

Who can get it

It can go to a surviving spouse who lived with him. It can also go to a spouse who lived apart, if she qualifies for benefits on his record.

Whether you qualify in your own case is a question for Social Security, and it can go on the same call as everything else.

You have two years

You have two years from his death to apply. In the first weeks that sounds like forever, which is how it gets forgotten.

If it doesn’t come up on your first call, write it down for later. You can ask about it at the same appointment as your survivor benefits.

How to ask for it

Survivor benefits can’t be applied for online. You call Social Security at 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778) or visit a local office. Getting an appointment can take weeks, which is why the call is worth making early. Booking one decides nothing.

Questions to bring:

  • Has his death been reported to you?
  • Can I apply for the $255 payment at the same appointment as survivor benefits?
  • Can I get survivor benefits for the month he died?
  • What should I bring?

Reporting the death is a separate step, and a payment of his for the month he died has to go back. Both are in how to tell Social Security that someone has died.

Other money that may be paid at a death

The $255 may not be the only payment. Each of these comes from a different place, and each has to be asked for.

  • Monthly survivor benefits from Social Security, a pension, federal or military service, or the VA. What exists and what to ask each office is in survivor benefits after a husband dies.
  • Life insurance. If you don’t know whether he had a policy, the free Life Insurance Policy Locator from the NAIC asks participating insurers to search. You enter details from the death certificate, and if you are the beneficiary, the company contacts you. More in How do I claim the life insurance?
  • A VA burial allowance. If he served and wasn’t dishonorably discharged, VA may pay toward funeral and burial costs when certain conditions apply. Two examples: a service-connected death, or VA pension or compensation at the time he died. For a death not connected to service, file within two years of the burial. Some eligible spouses are paid automatically. VA benefits: 800-827-1000.

If someone calls offering to help

People pretend to be Social Security. On Social Security’s own list of scam signs are a caller who threatens arrest, demands payment at once, or tells you to keep it secret.

So is one who wants gift cards, prepaid cards, a wire transfer, cryptocurrency, gold or cash in the mail, or offers to meet you to collect it.

You can hang up and call the number above yourself. You can report it at oig.ssa.gov/report. More in Who is trying to scam me?
