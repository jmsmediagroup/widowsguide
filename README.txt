WIDOWSGUIDE.ORG: THE SITE
A plain static site. No database, no server code. Upload this folder as it is.

HOW IT IS MADE
  Every page is generated from the books by one script:
      python3 09_site/build_site.py        (run from the project folder)
  It reads each book's book.json, its chapters (title, question, short answer)
  and the description in its KDP-listing.md. Edit the script, not the HTML,
  then run it again. It keeps the Amazon links you pasted into assets/books.js.

PAGES
  /                  home: what the books are, "How long has it been?", all eight
  /books/            the eight books, with the "How long has it been?" picker
  /books/<title>/    one page per book: description, every chapter with its
                     short answer, buy (or "tell me when it's out"), next book
  /questions/        all 96 chapter questions, each linking to its own page
  /books/<title>/<question>/  one page per chapter question (96): short answer,
                     what to do, what can wait, who can help; Article + FAQ data
  /words/            glossary from the books (DefinedTermSet data)
  /llms.txt          plain map of the site for AI search engines
  /start/            the free checklist (download, no sign-up needed)
  /first-30-days/    landing page for search ads: the checklist first, then Book 1
  /for-family/       which book to give, and what helps besides a book
  /about/            the author, how the books are written and checked
  /help-tonight/     crisis lines (also in every page footer)
  /contact/ /privacy/ /terms/
  /thanks/           shown after a sign-up (the conversion page for ads)
  /1/ ... /8/        printed with QR codes inside the books, and linked from every
                     "Book N" mention in the Kindle editions. Each goes to that
                     book's Amazon page once its link is in assets/books.js;
                     until then, to the book's page here, with the sign-up ready.
  /next/             old address; forwards to /books/

WHEN A BOOK IS PUBLISHED
  Open assets/books.js and paste its Amazon link into  amazon: ""  for that
  book (the Kindle page, amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>; KDP links the paperback and
  hardcover to it). Then run  python3 09_site/build_site.py  and upload the site again. Every printed QR code, short
  address and Kindle link in the books already published now leads there,
  and every "Tell me when it's out" button for that book becomes "Buy".

HOSTING
  Netlify: drag this folder onto app.netlify.com/drop, then point widowsguide.org
  at it. _redirects, _headers and netlify.toml are read by Netlify.
  Don't upload _source/ publicly if your host serves it (robots.txt blocks it).

THE EMAIL SIGN-UP (MAILERLITE)
  Every sign-up form posts to /api/subscribe, which is netlify/functions/
  subscribe.mjs. That function adds the address to MailerLite and sends her to
  /thanks/. No MailerLite script or stylesheet is loaded on any page, so the
  design and the page speed are unchanged, and the form works without
  JavaScript. The API key is never in the site; it is a Netlify environment
  variable. Both files are generated by build_site.py (MAILERLITE = True).

  One-time setup, in this order:
   1. MailerLite > Subscribers > Groups: create a group, e.g. "Widowsguide.org".
      Open it and copy the group ID out of the address bar.
      (The account also holds older subscribers from another project, so a
      group of its own keeps the two lists apart.)
   2. MailerLite > Subscribers > Fields: add a text field named exactly
      "interest". It records which page she signed up from, such as book-2 or
      checklist. If you skip this, sign-ups still work; the note is dropped.
   3. MailerLite > Integrations > Developer API: generate a token and copy it.
   4. Netlify > Site configuration > Environment variables, add:
          MAILERLITE_API_KEY    the token from step 3
          MAILERLITE_GROUP_ID   the group ID from step 1 (optional)
      Then redeploy, because a function only picks up new variables on a
      deploy.
   5. Sign up on the live site with your own address and check it arrives in
      the group. If something is wrong you land on /thanks/?ok=0, which says
      so and asks her to email instead. The reason is in Netlify > Logs >
      Functions.
   6. MailerLite > Forms is not used, and neither is Netlify Forms. Export any
      addresses already sitting in Netlify > Forms before you forget them.

  New subscribers are added straight to the list, with no confirmation email
  (status "active"). Every campaign still needs its unsubscribe link, which
  MailerLite puts in by default. Keep it.

  To go back to Netlify Forms, set MAILERLITE = False at the top of
  build_site.py and rebuild. Delete netlify.toml and netlify/ by hand.

ADS AND MEASUREMENT
  Google Analytics 4 is live (G-EDF55RBE1L, set at the top of build_site.py,
  23 Oct 2026). It records page views plus four events:
      sign_up              the /thanks/ page loaded after a sign-up that worked
      checklist_download   a click on the checklist PDF
      click_amazon         a click on any Buy link
      short_link_open      someone opened a printed short link, /1/ to /8/ or
                           /next/, usually by scanning a QR code in a book.
                           The "link" parameter says which (book-1 ... book-8).
  GA4's own "enhanced measurement" also records file_download for the PDF; the
  site's checklist_download is the same click under its own name, so use one.
  The short links wait for their hit to be sent (at most 0.9 s) before moving
  on, so a blocked or slow analytics script never leaves her on a blank page.

  1. Create a Google Analytics 4 property and a Google Ads account.
  2. Put the IDs at the top of 09_site/build_site.py (GA4_ID, ADS_ID and the
     three conversion labels: sign-up, checklist download, click to Amazon),
     run the script, upload. The privacy page then describes the tracking.
  3. Point ads at /first-30-days/ (people searching for what to do after a
     husband dies) and /for-family/ (people buying for someone).
  4. Use keyword search ads. Google restricts ads personalized on "personal
     hardships", which includes bereavement, so don't build remarketing or
     audience lists around grief, and don't write ad text that implies you know
     the reader's situation ("Did your husband just die?"). Check Google's
     current policy before launching.
  Visits to /2/ ... /8/ count print read-through: a reader who finished one book
  and looked for the next.

BEFORE IT GOES LIVE
  1. Register widowsguide.org (the .com is taken) and point it at the host.
  2. Paste each Amazon link into assets/books.js as the books publish.
  3. Re-check the phone numbers on the checklist and in the footer against the
     agencies' own pages.
  4. Have the privacy and terms pages read by someone qualified before you
     collect emails at scale or run ads.

THE CHECKLIST PDF
  Source: _source/checklist.html (identical to Book 1's printed checklist).
  Re-render after edits:
      "/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" --headless
        --no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf=out.pdf _source/checklist.html
  then copy it to assets/the-first-two-weeks.pdf and
  00_templates/04_lead-magnet/the-first-two-weeks.pdf.

DESIGN NOTES
  Built for a reader in her sixties on a phone: 19 to 21px body text, 52 to
  64px buttons, high contrast, no pop-ups, nothing that moves. The checklist
  is free with no email required; sign-up is offered alongside, never as a gate.

PERFORMANCE
  Fonts are served from assets/fonts (no Google Fonts). The stylesheet is inlined
  in every page by build_site.py. Covers are WebP in four sizes with a JPEG
  fallback. _headers sets caching and basic security headers on Netlify.
  Lighthouse, mobile, 19 Oct 2026: 99 to 100 on performance, 100 on
  accessibility, best practices and SEO.

SEARCH AND AI SEARCH (SEO / GEO)
  - Every chapter question has its own page with the answer first, lists, a
    visible breadcrumb and structured data (Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList).
  - Book pages list the agencies the facts were checked against.
  - robots.txt explicitly allows search and AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
    PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and others); llms.txt maps the site for them.
  - After the domain is live: add it to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster
    Tools (Bing feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot) and submit /sitemap.xml in both.
    Google is verified by a DNS TXT record; Bing by BingSiteAuth.xml, which
    build_site.py writes from BING_VERIFY.
  - IndexNow: after every deploy, run
        python3 09_site/indexnow.py
    It tells Bing, Yandex, Seznam and Naver which pages changed, instead of
    waiting for them to crawl. Google does not take part. Pass paths to send
    only some (python3 09_site/indexnow.py /start/ /1/), or --check to test the
    key alone. The key lives in build_site.py as INDEXNOW_KEY and is published
    at /<key>.txt. Run it after the deploy is live, never before: the engines
    fetch each URL, so a page that is not up yet is a wasted submission.
