Contact

Corrections, questions about the books, and requests to share the free checklist.

Write to hello@widowsguide.org.

That address is for corrections, questions about the books, and requests from hospices, churches or support groups that want to share the free checklist. To stop the emails, use the unsubscribe link in any of them, or write to the same address.
