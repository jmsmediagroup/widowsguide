Letters to send after your husband dies

Eight letters you can fill in once, then copy or print. Each says what to put in the envelope and what the answer will tell you.

The letters, roughly in the order they come up

  1. A credit bureau: One letter to one bureau is enough. It records his death on his credit file, and that bureau tells the other two.
  2. Credit card companies and lenders: It tells them he died, asks what is owed, and asks how the account was held, with the answers in writing.
  3. His bank: It asks the bank how each account is owned, because that decides what happens to it, and what the bank needs from you.
  4. A life insurance company: It asks for the claim forms, and for the ways the money can be paid before you choose one.
  5. His employer: If he was working, his employer may hold several things at once: pay, insurance, a retirement plan, and your health coverage.
  6. His pension plan: It asks for the plan’s own description of the survivor benefit, in writing, which is the document that answers most questions.
  7. Phone, utility and other accounts: For the accounts that keep the house running: the phone, gas, electric, water, internet and subscriptions.
  8. A debt collector who has written: For when a collector writes. Sent within 30 days, it makes the collector stop until it verifies the debt.

Most of these can also be done by phone, and some companies have a page for reporting a death online. A letter gives you a record of what you said and when, and asks for the answer in writing.

You are

What you type stays in this browser tab and is never sent anywhere. Don’t type Social Security or account numbers here. Write those into the letter yourself, on screen or by hand after printing.

What almost every letter needs

A copy of the death certificate. Most places take a photocopy, and some ask for a certified copy. Ask before you send a certified one. On how many to order, see How many death certificates do I need?

If you are the executor, some will also ask for the court papers that appoint you.

Nothing on this site asks for his Social Security number or any account number. Write those into the letter yourself.

Calls, not letters

Social Security is a phone call or a visit: see how to tell Social Security that someone has died and the $255 death payment. The free checklist for the first two weeks puts the calls and the letters in order.

General information, not legal or financial advice. Each rule on this page was checked in October 2026 against the agency or company it comes from. A lawyer licensed in your state can tell you how it applies to you.

If today is very bad: call or text 988, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org, any hour. It is for anyone who is struggling; you do not have to be suicidal to reach out.

Veterans Crisis Line, also for family and friends: dial 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741. In an emergency, call 911.
