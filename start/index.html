Free checklist

The First Two Weeks

A free checklist you can print. It lists what has to happen in the first fourteen days, in order, and what can wait.

Two pages, nothing to sign up for

Print it, or ask someone to print it for you. A librarian at your public library can help too. Check off what’s done. Skip what doesn’t apply.

Open the checklist (PDF, 2 pages)

You are welcome to print copies for a friend, a hospice or a support group.

What’s on it

  • What to do in the first two days
  • The six calls for the first week, starting with the funeral home and Social Security
  • What to find and set up by the end of week two
  • What can wait, so you can set it aside for now
  • A page for your numbers, with Social Security and the 988 crisis line already printed
  • A log for every call: the date, who you spoke to, their number, a reference number, and what they need

The book that goes with it

The checklist comes from Book 1, The First 30 Days. The book explains each step: what it is for, what to ask, and who can answer.

Tell me when it’s out

If you’d like, a few emails later on

Five or six short emails a year at most, and a note when a new book is out. Each one is about something that tends to come up in the first two years. You don’t need to sign up to get the checklist.

For readers in the United States. We don’t sell or rent your address, and every email has a link to unsubscribe. How we handle your email

If today is very bad: call or text 988, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org, any hour. It is for anyone who is struggling; you do not have to be suicidal to reach out.

Veterans Crisis Line, also for family and friends: dial 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741. In an emergency, call 911.
